# FTSE 100 Warren Buffett Screener — Column Header Dictionary

This document provides a comprehensive reference for all column headers in the **FTSE 100 Warren Buffett Value Investing Screener** spreadsheet.

---

## 1. Company Identification

| Column Header | Data Type | Formula / Source | Description & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ticker** | String | London Stock Exchange Ticker (e.g. `SHEL.L`) | The unique EPIC symbol for the company on the LSE. On Google Sheets, prefix with `LON:` (e.g. `LON:SHEL`). |
| **Company Name** | String | `yfinance` / LSE Registry | Official corporate name of the security. |
| **Sector** | String | Global Industry Classification (GICS) | The broad economic sector (e.g., Financial Services, Basic Materials, Energy, Healthcare). |
| **Industry** | String | GICS Sub-industry | Specific industry classification (e.g., Life Insurance, Integrated Oil & Gas). |

---

## 2. Market & Valuation Multiples

| Column Header | Units | Formula / Source | Warren Buffett Target | Interpretation & Importance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price (£)** | GBP (£) | `=GOOGLEFINANCE("LON:" & Ticker, "price")` / 100 | N/A | Current market price per share in British Pounds (£). Normalized from pence (GBp). |
| **Market Cap (£B)** | GBP (£ Billion) | `=Price * Shares / 1e9` | N/A | Total equity valuation of the business. Buffett prefers established market leaders. |
| **P/E Ratio** | Ratio | `=Price / Earnings_Per_Share` | **<= 15.0 to 20.0** | Price-to-Earnings Ratio. Indicates how much investors pay per £1 of net profit. Lower P/E means higher earnings yield. |
| **P/B Ratio** | Ratio | `=Price / Book_Value_Per_Share` | **<= 2.0 to 2.5** | Price-to-Book Ratio. Compares market valuation to net asset value on balance sheet. |
| **PEG Ratio** | Ratio | `=(P/E Ratio) / (Annual EPS Growth Rate)` | **<= 1.0 to 1.5** | Price/Earnings-to-Growth Ratio. Measures whether stock price is reasonable relative to earnings growth. |

---

## 3. Moat & Profitability Metrics

| Column Header | Units | Formula / Source | Warren Buffett Target | Interpretation & Importance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ROE (%)** | Percentage | `=(Net Income / Shareholders Equity) * 100` | **>= 15.0%** | Return on Equity. Buffett's #1 favorite metric! Measures management's efficiency at generating profits on reinvested shareholder capital. |
| **Net Margin (%)** | Percentage | `=(Net Profit / Total Revenue) * 100` | **>= 10.0%** | Net Profit Margin. Percentage of revenue converted into bottom-line profit. High margins indicate pricing power and an economic moat. |
| **Op Margin (%)** | Percentage | `=(Operating Profit / Total Revenue) * 100` | **>= 15.0%** | Operating Profit Margin. Core business profitability before interest and taxes. |

---

## 4. Financial Health & Balance Sheet Safety

| Column Header | Units | Formula / Source | Warren Buffett Target | Interpretation & Importance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Debt/Equity** | Ratio | `=(Total Interest-Bearing Debt / Total Equity)` | **<= 0.50** | Leverage ratio. Buffett avoids companies burdened by excessive debt load to prevent insolvency during economic downturns. |
| **Current Ratio** | Ratio | `=(Current Assets / Current Liabilities)` | **>= 1.50** | Liquidity ratio. Measures ability to cover short-term liabilities with liquid assets. |
| **FCF Yield (%)** | Percentage | `=(Free Cash Flow / Market Cap) * 100` | **>= 5.0%** | Free Cash Flow Yield. Real cash generated by business available for dividend payout, share buybacks, or debt repayment. |

---

## 5. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) & Intrinsic Value

| Column Header | Units | Formula / Source | Warren Buffett Target | Interpretation & Importance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intrinsic Value (£)** | GBP (£) | `10-Year Discounted FCF Model + Terminal Value` | **> Market Price** | Estimated true economic value of one share based on future cash flows discounted at 9% hurdle rate. |
| **Margin of Safety (%)**| Percentage | `=((Intrinsic Value - Price) / Intrinsic Value) * 100` | **>= 20.0%** | Benjamin Graham & Warren Buffett's core principle. Buying at a discount to intrinsic value to protect capital against errors or bad market cycles. |

---

## 6. Composite Scoring & Decision Matrix

| Column Header | Units | Formula / Source | Target Range | Signal Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buffett Score** | Points (0–100) | Sum of Quality (25), Health (25), Valuation (25), MOS (25) | **75 – 100**: Outstanding<br>**62 – 74**: Good<br>**48 – 61**: Average<br>**< 48**: Poor | Composite score combining Moat, Balance Sheet, Valuation, and Margin of Safety. |
| **Signal** | Categorical | Decision Algorithm Matrix | **STRONG BUY**<br>**BUY**<br>**HOLD**<br>**SELL** | **STRONG BUY**: Score >= 75 AND MOS > 20% AND Low Debt.<br>**BUY**: Score >= 62 AND MOS > 0%.<br>**HOLD**: Score 48 to 61.<br>**SELL**: Score < 48 or high debt/negative earnings. |

---

## 7. Time Series & Historical Validation Columns

| Column Header | Units | Formula / Source | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1Y Return (%)** | Percentage | `=(Price_Today - Price_1Y_Ago) / Price_1Y_Ago * 100` | 1-Year trailing total price return. |
| **3Y Return (%)** | Percentage | `=(Price_Today - Price_3Y_Ago) / Price_3Y_Ago * 100` | 3-Year trailing total price return. |
| **5Y Return (%)** | Percentage | `=(Price_Today - Price_5Y_Ago) / Price_5Y_Ago * 100` | 5-Year trailing total price return. |
| **Historical Score 2021**| Points (0–100) | Reconstructed 2021 Fundamentals | Model Buffett Score evaluated on 2021 financial statements. |
| **Model Outperformance**| Percentage | `=Stock_3Y_Return - Benchmark_3Y_Return` | Excess return generated over the FTSE 100 index benchmark (`ISF.L`). |
